Over the past few months, we've collected feedback from our users in customer conversations and feedback calls, and analyzed how vendors use Distr in their day-to-day operations. We looked at how teams navigate the platform, which actions they perform most frequently and how they manage their customers and software deployments.

As more of our customers grow and manage an increasing number of deployments across different environments, it became clear that our existing dashboard was no longer providing the level of visibility they needed. Managing dozens of deployments requires a different approach than managing just a handful.

With [Distr 4.11](https://github.com/distr-sh/distr/releases/tag/4.11.0), we're introducing a new Vendor Portal experience based on these insights.

Our goal was to make navigation more intuitive, simplify everyday workflows and give vendors better visibility into their customers and deployments, regardless of how many customers they serve.

Here's what's new.

## An actionable dashboard

The redesigned dashboard gives you an immediate overview of your customers and deployments and highlights where your attention is needed.

Rather than displaying every individual deployment, the new dashboard focuses on the information that matters most to vendors when managing their customers at scale.

From a single page, you can now see:

- Failing or unresponsive deployments
- Customers running or pulling outdated versions of your software
- Expired, expiring or missing licenses
- Open customer support requests
- Unresolved security advisories

Each card takes you directly to the relevant section of Distr with the appropriate filters already applied, allowing you to quickly investigate and address issues.

The dashboard also provides an overview of your customers and their current status, alongside a history of recent events across your organization.

## Everything you need to manage your customers in one place

We've significantly improved how vendors manage their customers in Distr.

Previously, managing customer users, customizing the Customer Portal, enabling or disabling features and managing licenses meant jumping between several pages.

Each customer now has a dedicated detail page where you can access all relevant information and manage their:

- Users and access permissions
- Deployments
- Registry downloads
- Secrets
- Licenses and license keys
- Notifications
- Support bundles

You can also directly customize the customer's portal experience, manage which features are available to them and configure their individual settings without leaving the customer detail page.

We've also improved the customer list with search and filtering options for update and license status, making it easier to identify customers who require your attention.

Whether you're onboarding a new customer, adjusting their access to your software or supporting an existing customer, you can now do so from a single place.

## Better visibility and filtering across all your deployments

We've improved the deployments page to make it easier to find and monitor deployments across all your customers and environments.

Instead of grouping deployments by customer, the page now provides a unified list of all your deployments, with quicker access to relevant filters.

You can filter deployments by their current status, including the newly introduced "outdated" status, which helps you quickly identify deployments that are running older versions of your software.

Combined with the new dashboard, this makes it significantly easier to identify and investigate issues, especially as the number of deployments you manage grows.

## Keep track of what happened with the new activity history

Understanding what happened across your customers and deployments is just as important as knowing their current status.

With Distr 4.11, we're bringing activity history directly to the home pages of both the Vendor Portal and the Customer Portal.

Vendors can now follow recent events across their organization from a single view, while customers can see the activity relevant to their own environments.

This gives both vendors and customers a better understanding of what has happened, makes it easier to follow changes over time and provides additional context when investigating issues or supporting customers.

## Simplified navigation and organization settings

As part of the new Vendor Portal experience, we've simplified the main navigation to make frequently used features more accessible.

Organization-specific settings, including team management, internal deployment secrets, custom domains, authentication, subscription and billing, are now grouped together under Settings.

## Get things done faster with the Command Palette

We've introduced several improvements to make navigating Distr and performing everyday tasks more efficient.

With the new Command Palette, accessible via `Cmd+K` on macOS or `Ctrl+K` on Windows and Linux, you can quickly navigate between different sections of the Vendor Portal or switch directly to a customer's portal.

We've also introduced keyboard shortcuts for frequently accessed pages, allowing you to navigate Distr even faster.

## Experience Distr from your customer's perspective with Customer Lens

When onboarding a customer or helping them troubleshoot an issue, it can be useful to see exactly what they see.

With [Customer Lens](/docs/platform/rbac/#customer-lens), you can directly access the Customer Portal of any of your customers using your existing role, without having to create a separate customer account or receive an invitation.

This is particularly useful when supporting customers, reviewing their onboarding experience or verifying which features and resources are available to them.

Partners can also use Customer Lens to access the portals of their assigned customers.

Customer Lens is accessible through the context switcher and the new Command Palette, making it easy to switch between your Vendor Portal and your customers' portals.

## An improved experience for your customers

The Vendor Portal isn't the only part of Distr receiving an update. We've also introduced a new home page for the [Customer Portal](/docs/platform/customer-portal/).

Your customers now have a dedicated starting point that brings together the information most relevant to them, including:

- Their deployments and current status
- Artifacts available through your registry
- Your customized onboarding instructions and documentation
- License information
- Open support requests
- Update notification reminders
- Recent activity and history

The information displayed is tailored to the features you've enabled for each customer and how they consume your software.

New customers are also guided through their first steps, such as inviting their team, creating their first deployment or downloading an artifact.

This makes the Customer Portal more intuitive for both newly onboarded and existing customers, helping them get started, stay informed and manage their software independently.

All existing Customer Portal functionality remains available.

## Upgrading to Distr 4.11

The new Vendor Portal experience is available to all Distr Cloud users. If you're self-hosting Distr, you can upgrade to version 4.11 as you would with any other minor release.

No additional configuration is required, and no new environment variables have been introduced. The SDK and controllers remain unchanged, so your existing integrations and customer deployments will continue to work as before.

We do recommend adding a title image to each of your applications on its application page. The Customer Portal home page shows it next to your customers' deployments and artifacts.

As part of the updated navigation, some standalone pages have been consolidated into other sections of the platform. Previously bookmarked pages, such as the standalone licenses page, may therefore need to be updated.

The only removed API endpoint is the one associated with the previous dashboard.
