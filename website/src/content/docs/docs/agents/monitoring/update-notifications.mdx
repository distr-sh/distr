Update notifications tell selected users by email that a new version is available: a new version of an application whose deployments are still on an older one, or a new version of an artifact in the registry.

<Aside type="note">
  Update notifications are a **Pro feature** and require a [Pro
  subscription](/pricing/).
</Aside>

## How to Configure Update Notifications

In the sidebar, navigate to **Notifications** > **Updates**.

1. Click **Update Notification**.
2. Enter a name for the configuration.
3. Switch between **Applications** and **Artifacts**, and select what to watch. One configuration can watch applications and artifacts together, so an application and the images it is shipped with are announced from a single place.
4. Select the users who should receive the emails. Your own team, your partners and your customers' users can all be picked.

Each configuration can be toggled on or off without deleting it, and the **History** page lists what has been sent.

## Configuring for a Customer

The page lists what your own organization has configured. **Show customer updates** leads to the same page for one of your customers, where the breadcrumb switches between them, and the **Alerts**, **Updates** and **History** of a customer can be managed the way their users and secrets are.

A configuration created there belongs to the customer: it can only watch what that customer is [entitled](/docs/platform/license-management/) to and only notify users of their organization.

## Letting Customers Configure Their Own

Grant a customer organization the **Update Notifications** feature in **Customers** to let its admins decide who of their own users hears about a new version. They reach the page through the **Updates** button on their deployments and artifacts pages, and can only pick the applications and artifacts they are entitled to and users of their own organization.

## When a Notification Is Sent

An application notification is sent when a new version of a watched application is created, to every selected recipient who can see at least one deployment that does not run it yet. A customer is left out while their [entitlement](/docs/platform/license-management/) does not cover the new version, and hears about it once the entitlement is widened.

An artifact notification is sent when a new tag is pushed to a watched artifact. Artifacts that mirror an upstream registry cannot be watched, because all of their versions appear at once when the mirror syncs.

Each recipient hears about a version once, however many deployments are affected and however many configurations cover them. A customer who watches the same application as you do receives one email, not two.

## Notification Emails

Every email contains the application or artifact, its type and logo, the new version and a link to Distr.

A recipient from your own team or a partner organization sees every affected customer and deployment in one email. A customer user sees only their own deployments.
