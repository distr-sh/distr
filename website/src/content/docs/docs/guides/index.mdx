These guides walk you through distributing your application with Distr onto specific platforms and through common delivery workflows, from what you set up as a vendor to what your customer does in their own environment.

## Deployment Guides

- **[Amazon ECS](/docs/guides/aws-ecs/)** - Run your application on Amazon ECS in your customers' AWS accounts, with images pulled from the Distr registry
- **[Blue-Green Deployments](/docs/guides/blue-green-deployments/)** - Run two versions side by side on a Docker or Kubernetes deployment target and shift traffic between them without downtime
