Customer secrets hold the credentials of one customer's deployments, for example the customer's database password or an API key specific to its environment. Distr injects them only into that customer's deployments. Other customers and your internal deployments cannot use them.

## Who manages them

A customer's secrets are stored once and shared by everyone who manages that customer:

- **You**, in the **Secrets** tab of the customer page in the Vendor Portal.
- **The customer's users**, under **Secrets** in the [Customer Portal](/docs/platform/customer-portal/secrets/).
- **The partner** the customer is assigned to, if you work with partners.

A secret created in one of these places shows up in the others. Everyone sees the keys and who updated a secret last, never the values: Distr does not show a value again once it is saved. Users with the [role](/docs/platform/rbac/) Administrator or User can create, change and delete secrets. Viewers only see the list.

## Creating a secret

1. In the Vendor Portal sidebar, go to **Customers** and open the customer.
2. Select the **Secrets** tab and click **Create Secret**.
3. Enter a **Secret Key**, for example `DATABASE_PASSWORD`. It has to start with a letter and can contain only letters, numbers and underscores.
4. Enter the **Secret Value** and click **Submit**.

The **Reference** column shows the template to put into the customer's deployment configuration, for example `{{ .Secrets.DATABASE_PASSWORD }}`, with a button to copy it. See [Using secrets in deployments](/docs/controllers/secrets/#using-secrets-in-deployments) for where a reference works.

## Changing a value

1. Click the secret in the list.
2. Enter the new value. The key cannot be changed.
3. Click **Submit**.

When deployments use the secret, the form lists the deployment targets that will be redeployed with the new value, and the button reads **Save and redeploy**.

## Deleting a secret

Click the delete button next to the secret and confirm by typing its key. Distr does not delete a secret that a deployment still references and names the deployment targets that use it instead. Remove the reference from their configuration first.

## Internal deployment secrets

Credentials that only your own deployments need, the ones on deployment targets without a customer, belong in your [internal deployment secrets](/docs/platform/internal-deployment-secrets/) in the **Secrets** tab of your settings. They work the same way, but Distr never injects them into customer deployments.
