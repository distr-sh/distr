A partner is an organization that serves customers on your behalf, such as a reseller or a distribution partner. You assign customers to it, and its users manage those customers in Distr: they onboard them, issue their licenses, deploy for them and answer their support requests. Your applications, artifacts and settings stay yours.

<Aside type="note">
  Partner management is part of the [Business plan](/docs/subscription/).
</Aside>

## Creating a partner

1. In the Vendor Portal sidebar, go to **Partners**.
2. Click **Create Partner** and enter the partner's name.
3. Click **Manage users** next to the partner and invite its users with their name, email and [role](/docs/platform/rbac/).

The list shows how many users and customers each partner has. Rename a partner with the edit icon next to its name.

## Assigning customers

Open a customer and pick the partner in the selector below its name, or **No partner** to manage the customer yourself again. When you move a customer away from a partner, that partner's users can no longer manage it.

Customers that a partner creates are assigned to that partner automatically.

## What partners see

Partner users sign in to the same domain as your team, where Distr shows them a **Partner Portal**. Its sidebar has **Home**, **Deployments**, **Registry**, **Support**, **Security**, **Customers** and, for partner administrators, **Team**.

Everything in it is limited to the customers assigned to the partner:

- **Customers**: they create customers, invite customer users, manage the customers' secrets, configure [alerts](/docs/controllers/alerts/) for them and open a customer's portal with [Customer Lens](/docs/platform/customer-lens/).
- **Licenses**: they manage the [application entitlements](/docs/platform/application-entitlements/), [artifact entitlements](/docs/platform/artifact-entitlements/) and [license keys](/docs/platform/license-keys/) of their customers.
- **Deployments**: they see and create deployments for their customers, but no internal deployments.
- **Registry**: they browse your artifacts and see the download history of their customers.
- **Support and security**: they see the [support requests](/docs/platform/support-bundles/) of their customers and read your published [security advisories](/docs/platform/vulnerability-management/), with the impact on their customers.

Partners cannot create or change applications or artifacts, push to the registry, write advisories or change your organization's settings or branding. Partner administrators can delete their own customers.

## Subscription and billing

- Every partner user takes a user seat of your organization, like a member of your own team.
- Every customer counts toward your customer limit, whether you or a partner created it.
- The number of partners is not limited.

See [Subscription Management](/docs/subscription-management/) for how seats and customers are billed.

## Deleting a partner

Click the delete button next to the partner and confirm by typing its name. Its users lose access, and its customers are assigned back to you.
