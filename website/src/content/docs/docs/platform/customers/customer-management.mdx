The **Customers** section in the [Vendor Portal](/docs/vendor-portal/) is where you create and manage customer accounts, invite customer users, manage customer-specific secrets, configure links, and control which features each customer can use. This guide walks you through the customer management interface and common tasks.

<ThemedImage
  lightSrc={customerManagementLight}
  darkSrc={customerManagementDark}
  alt="Customers section in the Vendor Portal"
/>

## Overview

From the Customers page you can:

- **Create customers** and give them a name
- **Invite customer users** (name, email, and [role](/docs/platform/rbac/)) and manage access per customer
- **Manage licenses** for a specific customer in its **Licenses** tab. See [License Management](/docs/platform/license-management/) for entitlements and license keys
- **Manage secrets** for a specific customer in its **Secrets** tab. See [Customer Secrets](/docs/platform/customer-secrets/)
- **Manage links** that appear in that customer's [Customer Portal](/docs/platform/customer-portal/) sidebar
- **Enable or disable features** per customer: Deployments, [Alerts](/docs/controllers/alerts/), Artifacts, [Update Notifications](/docs/controllers/update-notifications/), [Support](/docs/platform/support-bundles/) and [Security](/docs/platform/vulnerability-management/)

The list shows each customer's deployments, update state, license and support requests at a glance. Use the **Filter customers** search bar and the update state and license filters to find customers, and **+ Create Customer** to add a new one.

## Creating a customer

1. In the Vendor Portal sidebar, go to **Customers**.
2. Click **+ Create Customer**.
3. Enter a name for the customer.
4. Confirm to create the customer.

The new customer appears in the list. You can then invite users, manage secrets, and configure features for them.

## The customer page

Click a customer in the list to open its page.

<ThemedImage
  lightSrc={customerDetailLight}
  darkSrc={customerDetailDark}
  alt="Customer page with the overview tab showing the customer's deployments, updates, licenses, support, activity and users"
/>

The customer page brings together everything about one customer in tabs:

- **Overview** shows the same attention cards as the home page of the [Vendor Portal](/docs/vendor-portal/) for this customer, along with its activity and users.
- **Users** lists the customer's users. [Invite](#inviting-customer-users) new users and assign their roles here.
- **Deployments** shows the customer's deployments and their status.
- **Registry** shows which artifact versions the customer pulled.
- **Secrets** holds the [secrets](/docs/platform/customer-secrets/) the customer's deployments use.
- **Licenses** holds the customer's [application entitlements](/docs/platform/application-entitlements/), [artifact entitlements](/docs/platform/artifact-entitlements/) and [license keys](/docs/platform/license-keys/).
- **Notifications** configures the [alerts](/docs/controllers/alerts/) and [update notifications](/docs/controllers/update-notifications/) for this customer and lists the notifications sent.
- **Support** lists the customer's support requests.
- **Settings** controls which [features the customer can use](#enabling-and-disabling-features-per-customer) and which [links](#managing-links-per-customer) appear in its sidebar.

**Open customer portal** shows the Customer Portal exactly as this customer sees it, through [Customer Lens](/docs/platform/customer-lens/).

## Inviting customer users

1. In the **Customers** list, open the customer you want to add users to.
2. Select the **Users** tab.
3. Click **Add User**.
4. Enter **name**, **email**, and **role** (see [RBAC](/docs/platform/rbac/) for Administrator, User, and Viewer).
5. Submit the invitation.

The invited user receives an email to set up their account. The **first user** for a customer is always an **Administrator**. After that, customer admins can add or remove users themselves from the [Customer Portal](/docs/platform/customer-portal/).

  The full steps and role options for inviting customer users are described in

## Managing links per customer

You can add links for each customer, for example to your documentation, a support page, or a status page. They are shown at the bottom of the sidebar in that customer's [Customer Portal](/docs/platform/customer-portal/) and open in a new tab.

To add a link:

1. In the **Customers** list, open the customer.
2. Select the **Settings** tab.
3. In the **Sidebar Links** panel, click **+ Add link** and enter a **name** and the **link**.

## Enabling and disabling features per customer

You can control which features are available to each customer organization. The available features are:

- **Deployments**: access to controller-based deployments
- **Alerts**: deployment health notifications (requires Deployments)
- **Artifacts**: access to the artifact registry
- **Update Notifications**: deciding which of their own users hears about a new application or artifact version
- **Support**: ability to create support requests and submit diagnostic bundles
- **Security**: access to the security advisories you publish (requires the Business plan)
- **Advanced Settings**: a settings page for the customer's administrators, where they choose the [controller image repository](/docs/platform/branding/#controller-image-repository) of their deployment targets (requires the Business plan)
- **Identity Provider**: a [portal domain and identity provider](/docs/platform/custom-identity-provider/#letting-a-customer-bring-its-own-provider) of the customer's own, configured on its settings page (requires Advanced Settings and the Business plan)

Open the customer and switch individual features on or off in the **Features** panel of its **Settings** tab. The tab of a disabled feature explains what that means for the customer and lets you enable the feature from there.

<ThemedImage
  lightSrc={featuresLight}
  darkSrc={featuresDark}
  alt="Features panel on the Settings tab of a customer with toggles for Deployments, Alerts, Artifacts, Update Notifications, Support, Security, Advanced Settings and Identity Provider"
/>

A few things to note:

- **Alerts** depends on **Deployments**. Enabling Alerts automatically enables Deployments; disabling Deployments automatically disables Alerts.
- **Identity Provider** depends on **Advanced Settings** in the same way.
- **Support** and **Artifacts** are independent and can be toggled without affecting other features.
- **Update Notifications** needs **Deployments** or **Artifacts** and covers whichever of them the customer has, since a customer can only watch what it can reach. Disabling the last of the two disables Update Notifications as well.
- **Deployments** and **Artifacts** are enabled by default for new customers. **Alerts**, **Update Notifications** and **Support** must be explicitly enabled.
- **Security** is enabled by default for new customers once your organization has [Vulnerability Management](/docs/platform/vulnerability-management/). When your organization gains it, every existing customer gets it as well. You can switch it off for individual customers afterwards.
- Changes take effect immediately. Disabled features are hidden entirely from the customer in the Customer Portal. Customers only see the sections that are enabled for them.
