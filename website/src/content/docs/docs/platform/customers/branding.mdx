The Branding page of the Vendor Portal allows you to apply your own branding to the UI that customers use to deploy and manage your software.

A well-configured Customer Portal should provide all the information your end users need to install, operate, and troubleshoot your software independently.

<Aside type="note">
  Custom branding for your Customer Portal is available on the Pro plan and
  above. Custom domains for a fully white-labeled experience require the
  Business plan. See the [pricing page](/pricing/) for a detailed comparison.
</Aside>

To get started, go to the Branding tab in the sidebar of the Vendor Portal.
At the top of the page, live previews show what the navigation bar will look like for your own team in the Vendor Portal and for your customers in the Customer Portal.

## Customer Portal

These settings configure what your customers see when they log in to their portal:

- **Customer Portal Override**: Optional. Replaces the "Customer Portal" text next to the logo in the customer navigation bar. If not set, your customers see "Customer Portal".
- **Welcome Page**: Markdown content shown to your customers on the home page of their portal. Recommended content:
  - Installation and setup instructions
  - Connection and upgrade instructions
  - Troubleshooting tips
  - Support contact details

<Aside type="tip">
  Release notes are best attached directly to the corresponding application
  version as [additional
  resources](/docs/agents/application/#additional-resources), so customers
  always see the notes for the version they are running.
</Aside>

After saving, the [Customer Portal](/docs/platform/customer-portal/) is rebranded for your end users:

## White-label

These settings replace Distr's branding across the application for users and customers of your organization:

- **Portal Title**: Shown as the browser tab title. If not set, the default Distr title is used.
- **Portal Logo**: Shown in the navigation bar for users and customers of your organization, and on the login page of your custom domain. SVG, PNG, JPG or GIF with a recommended height of 64px. If not set, the Distr logo is shown.
- **Favicon**: Shown in the browser tab. SVG, PNG, GIF or ICO with a recommended size of 64px x 64px (square). If not set, the Distr favicon is shown.

None of these settings require a custom domain: they apply to all logged-in users and customers of your organization on app.distr.sh as well. With a [custom domain](#custom-domains), they additionally apply to unauthenticated pages such as the login page.

## Custom Domains

For a fully white-labeled experience, Distr supports custom domains for the Customer Portal and the artifact registry.
When your portal is accessed via your custom domain, all Distr-specific branding is removed and your white-label settings also apply to unauthenticated pages such as the login page.

Organization admins can set up custom domains self-service under **Settings → Organization → Custom Domains**:

1. **App domain** (e.g. `app.your-company.com`): Serves the web UI and API, including the Customer Portal. It also serves the artifact registry under the `/v2/` path, so a single domain is usually all you need.
2. **Registry domain** (optional, e.g. `registry.your-company.com`): Only needed if you want a separate hostname for the artifact registry.

Enter the domain as a bare hostname (no `https://`) and add it.
Then create the CNAME record shown below the domain at your DNS provider, pointing your domain at the displayed target.
The TLS certificate is issued automatically on the first request once the DNS record has propagated — no certificate upload or renewal handling required.

<Aside type="note">
  Custom domains are available on the Business plan. Each domain can only be
  registered by one organization. Removing a domain makes it stop working
  immediately and its TLS certificate is no longer renewed.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Login methods on your custom domain">
  The Google, GitHub and Microsoft sign-in buttons are configured for the Distr
  instance as a whole, not for a single organization, so they are not offered on
  your domain. Visitors there sign in with email and password, or with a [custom
  identity provider](/docs/platform/custom-identity-provider/) you configure
  yourself. Self-registration is also disabled on your domain: new users are
  invited or provisioned by your identity provider.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip">
  A custom sender address for transactional e-mails is currently still
  configured by Distr support. Contact us at support@distr.sh for setup.
</Aside>

On self-hosted instances, the Custom Domains section only appears when the instance is configured for custom domain self-service.
See the [configuration reference](/docs/self-hosting/configuration/#custom-domains) for the required setup.
