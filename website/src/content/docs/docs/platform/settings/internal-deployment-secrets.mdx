Internal deployment secrets hold the credentials of your own deployments, the ones on deployment targets without a customer, for example a staging environment token or an API key for a test system. Distr never injects them into a customer's deployments. A credential that a customer deployment needs belongs in that customer's [secrets](/docs/platform/customer-secrets/).

## Who manages them

Internal deployment secrets belong to your organization alone. Partners and customers never see them. Everyone on your team sees the keys and who updated a secret last, never the values: Distr does not show a value again once it is saved. Members with the [role](/docs/platform/rbac/) Administrator or User can create, change and delete secrets. Viewers only see the list.

## Creating a secret

1. In the Vendor Portal sidebar, click **Settings** and open the **Secrets** tab.
2. Click **Create Secret**.
3. Enter a **Secret Key**, for example `STAGING_API_TOKEN`. It has to start with a letter and can contain only letters, numbers and underscores.
4. Enter the **Secret Value** and click **Submit**.

The **Reference** column shows the template to put into the deployment configuration, for example `{{ .Secrets.STAGING_API_TOKEN }}`, with a button to copy it. See [Using secrets in deployments](/docs/controllers/secrets/#using-secrets-in-deployments) for where a reference works.

## Changing a value

1. Click the secret in the list.
2. Enter the new value. The key cannot be changed.
3. Click **Submit**.

When deployments use the secret, the form lists the deployment targets that will be redeployed with the new value, and the button reads **Save and redeploy**.

## Deleting a secret

Click the delete button next to the secret and confirm by typing its key. Distr does not delete a secret that a deployment still references and names the deployment targets that use it instead. Remove the reference from their configuration first.
