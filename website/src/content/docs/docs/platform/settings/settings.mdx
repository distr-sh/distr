**Settings** in the [Vendor Portal](/docs/vendor-portal/) holds the configuration of your vendor organization. Open it from the sidebar or press `G` `S`.

<ThemedImage
  lightSrc={settingsLight}
  darkSrc={settingsDark}
  alt="Organization settings with the General tab open"
/>

- **General**: the organization name and registry [slug](/docs/registry/configuration/#1-set-your-registry-slug), registry and controller settings and deleting the organization.
- **Team**: the members of your vendor organization and their [roles](/docs/platform/rbac/).
- **Secrets**: the [secrets of your internal deployments](/docs/platform/internal-deployment-secrets/), the ones on deployment targets without a customer. A customer's secrets are on [its page](/docs/platform/customer-secrets/).
- **Domains & Sign-in**: [custom domains](/docs/platform/branding/#custom-domains), [identity providers](/docs/platform/custom-identity-provider/) and a custom email sender.
- **Subscription**: your plan, usage limits and billing information, see [Subscription Management](/docs/subscription-management/).
- **Billing**: license templates and the Stripe webhook, with vendor billing enabled.

<Aside type="note">
  Only admins see every tab. Other members see the **Secrets** tab.
</Aside>
