The Vendor Portal is the part of Distr your team works in. You publish [applications](/docs/controllers/application/) and [artifacts](/docs/registry/) there, manage your customers and their licenses, and follow every deployment in your customers' environments. Your customers work in the [Customer Portal](/docs/platform/customer-portal/), a simpler white-labeled interface that shows them only what you enable.

## The Vendor Portal at a glance

After you sign in, the Vendor Portal opens on **Home**.

<ThemedImage
  lightSrc={homeMapLight}
  darkSrc={homeMapDark}
  alt="Vendor Portal home page with numbered markers on the sidebar, the attention cards, the activity feed, the customers list, the color scheme toggle, the context switcher and the account menu"
/>

1. **Sidebar**: every section of the Vendor Portal. The upper group holds what you work with every day, the lower group the configuration you set up once. Each section is described [below](#distribution). Sections your plan does not include show a lock.
2. **Attention cards**: the deployments in error or without a recent status update, the customers running an older version, the expired or missing licenses, the open support requests and the open security advisories. Each card opens the matching list, already filtered.
3. **Activity**: what happened recently, such as deployments, new application versions, published artifacts, license changes and support requests.
4. **Customers**: your customers with the number of open items, so the ones that need attention come first. **View all** opens the [Customers](#customers) page.
5. **Color scheme**: switches between light and dark mode.
6. **Context switcher**: your organization and your role. Open it to switch to another organization or to look at a customer's portal with [Customer Lens](#customer-lens).
7. **Account menu**: update your profile, create [Personal Access Tokens](/docs/integrations/personal-access-token/) for the API, the SDK and the registry or sign out.

## Distribution

### Home

Home is the page shown above. Admins of an organization without a paid plan also see a **Getting started** card there, with guided tutorials for inviting teammates, the registry, controllers and branding.

### Applications

Create [Docker Compose and Helm applications](/docs/controllers/application/) and publish their versions. Each version can carry [resources](/docs/controllers/application/#additional-resources) such as release notes, a [vulnerability scan](/docs/platform/vulnerability-scanning/) or a [Kubernetes compatibility matrix](/docs/platform/kubernetes-compatibility-matrix/). The resources you mark as visible to customers appear in the Customer Portal.

### Partners

Create partner organizations that serve customers on your behalf, invite their users and see how many customers each partner has. Assign a customer to a partner on the customer's page. Partner users work with the customers assigned to them and can open their portals with [Customer Lens](/docs/platform/rbac/#customer-lens). The section appears when partner management is enabled for your organization.

### Customers

The **Customers** page lists all customers with their deployments, update status, license status and open support requests. [Create a customer](/docs/platform/customer-management/) here and click one to open its page.

<ThemedImage
  lightSrc={customerDetailLight}
  darkSrc={customerDetailDark}
  alt="Customer page with the overview tab showing the customer's deployments, updates, licenses, support, activity and users"
/>

The customer page brings together everything about one customer in tabs:

- **Overview** shows the same attention cards as the home page for this customer, along with its activity and users.
- **Users** lists the customer's users. [Invite](/docs/platform/rbac/#inviting-customer-organization-users) new users and assign their roles here.
- **Deployments** shows the customer's deployments and their status.
- **Registry** shows which artifact versions the customer pulled.
- **Secrets** holds the [secrets](/docs/controllers/secrets/) the customer's deployments use.
- **Licenses** holds the customer's [application entitlements](/docs/platform/application-entitlements/), [artifact entitlements](/docs/platform/artifact-entitlements/) and [license keys](/docs/platform/license-keys/).
- **Notifications** configures the alerts and update notifications for this customer and lists the notifications sent.
- **Support** lists the customer's support requests.
- **Settings** controls which [features the customer can use](/docs/platform/customer-management/#enabling-and-disabling-features-per-customer) and which [links](/docs/platform/customer-management/#managing-links-per-customer) appear in its sidebar.

**Open customer portal** shows the Customer Portal exactly as this customer sees it.

### Deployments

Follow every [deployment](/docs/controllers/#deployments) across all customers. Each deployment target shows the status of its deployments and the resource usage of its host or cluster. Filter by customer, application and status, open a target to read its logs, [update its controller](/docs/controllers/#controller-updates) from its menu and start a new deployment with **New Deployment**.

### Registry

Browse the artifacts in your [registry](/docs/registry/): container images, Helm charts and other OCI artifacts with their tags and the customers who pulled them. Add an artifact that [mirrors an upstream registry](/docs/registry/pull-through-cache/), and open **Downloads** for the [history of every pull](/docs/registry/analytics/), filterable by customer, user, deployment, address and artifact and exportable to CSV.

### Notifications

Configure [alerts](/docs/controllers/alerts/) for deployment status changes and resource usage, and [update notifications](/docs/controllers/update-notifications/) that email users when a new version is available. **Notification History** lists every notification sent.

### Support

Answer the support requests your customers open and review the diagnostic data in the [support bundles](/docs/platform/support-bundles/) they attach. **Settings** decides which environment variables, ConfigMap and Secret values and custom scripts a bundle collects, and which of them are redacted.

### Security

Track [security advisories](/docs/platform/vulnerability-management/) from triage to publication. Mark the affected and patched versions, see which customers deployed or pulled an affected version and publish the advisory to the customers who deployed or are entitled to one.

## Configuration

### Customer Portal

Configure what your customers see: the [branding](/docs/platform/branding/) of the Customer Portal with your title, logo and the instructions on its home page, and the domain and sign-in your customers use to reach it. Only members who may edit see this section.

### Settings

**Settings** holds the configuration of your vendor organization.

<ThemedImage
  lightSrc={settingsLight}
  darkSrc={settingsDark}
  alt="Organization settings with the General tab open"
/>

- **General**: the organization name, the registry [slug](/docs/registry/configuration/#1-set-your-registry-slug), registry and controller settings, and deleting the organization.
- **Team**: the members of your vendor organization and their [roles](/docs/platform/rbac/).
- **Secrets**: the [secrets](/docs/controllers/secrets/) of your internal deployments.
- **Domains & Sign-in**: [custom domains](/docs/platform/branding/#custom-domains), [identity providers](/docs/platform/custom-identity-provider/) and a custom email sender.
- **Subscription**: your plan, usage limits and billing information.
- **Billing**: license templates and the Stripe webhook, with vendor billing enabled.

<Aside type="note">
  Only admins see every tab. Other members see the **Secrets** tab.
</Aside>

## Customer Lens

Customer Lens lets you look at the Customer Portal of any customer without a separate account. Open the context switcher next to your organization name in the top-right corner and pick a customer.

<ThemedImage
  lightSrc={contextSwitcherLight}
  darkSrc={contextSwitcherDark}
  alt="Context switcher listing the customers you can open with Customer Lens"
/>

A banner at the top shows which customer you are looking at. Press `G` `P` or use the banner to return to the Vendor Portal. See [Customer Lens](/docs/platform/rbac/#customer-lens) for what you can do in a lens and who may use it.

## Command palette

Press `Ctrl` `K`, or `⌘` `K` on macOS, to open the command palette from anywhere in the Vendor Portal.

<ThemedImage
  lightSrc={commandPaletteLight}
  darkSrc={commandPaletteDark}
  alt="Command palette with applications and customers to jump to"
/>

Type to search across applications, customers, deployments and artifacts, or jump to any page in the sidebar. The palette also filters the deployments list by status, switches between your organizations and opens a customer in Customer Lens.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut              | Action                                              |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `Ctrl` `K` or `⌘` `K` | Open or close the command palette                   |
| `G` `H`               | Go to Home                                          |
| `G` `A`               | Go to Applications                                  |
| `G` `P`               | Go to Partners                                      |
| `G` `C`               | Go to Customers                                     |
| `G` `D`               | Go to Deployments                                   |
| `G` `R`               | Go to Registry                                      |
| `G` `N`               | Go to Notifications                                 |
| `G` `B`               | Go to Support                                       |
| `G` `V`               | Go to Security                                      |
| `G` `O`               | Go to Customer Portal                               |
| `G` `S`               | Go to Settings                                      |
| `G` `P` in a lens     | Leave Customer Lens and return to the Vendor Portal |
| `/`                   | Focus the search field of the current list          |

Press the second key within one and a half seconds after `G`. Shortcuts do nothing while you type in a form field.
