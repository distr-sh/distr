The Vendor Portal is the Distr UI for vendors. It gives you access to all Distr features, including [application creation](/docs/controllers/application/), [artifact registry](/docs/registry/configuration/) management, and monitoring of end-customer deployment states. The [Customer Portal](/docs/platform/customer-portal/) gives end customers a simpler interface where they configure and manage their deployments. It also centralizes release information and instructions for operating the vendor's software.

## Key Features

1. **Dashboard**: View health status for controllers and artifacts across all deployments at a glance.
2. **Controllers**: Add applications, create and manage deployments, set up [alerts](/docs/controllers/alerts/), monitor deployment health, and view deployment logs. See the [Controllers](/docs/controllers/) section for details.
3. **Registry**: Browse and view consumption metrics of your [artifacts](/docs/registry/) in the _Registry_ section. The **Downloads** button there opens the [download history](/docs/registry/analytics/) of every pull.
4. **Customers**: Create and manage customer organizations, invite users, manage secrets, and control features per customer. See the [Customer Management](/docs/platform/customer-management/) guide for a detailed walkthrough; to invite users and assign roles, see [RBAC: Inviting Customer Organization Users](/docs/platform/rbac/#inviting-customer-organization-users). The **Licenses** tab of a customer holds its [entitlements and license keys](/docs/platform/license-management/): Application Entitlements control which application versions the customer can deploy, Artifact Entitlements control registry access, and License Keys let you issue signed tokens your application can use to enforce usage limits and feature access.
5. **Support**: Receive support requests and review the diagnostic data customers attach to them. Customers run a one-time collection script against their Docker host or Kubernetes cluster, and the collected logs, configuration and resources appear here for review and discussion. See [Support Bundles](/docs/platform/support-bundles/) for details.
6. **Customer Portal**: [White-label](/docs/platform/branding/) the customer portal with your branding and provide customers with self-service instructions and documentation in the _Branding_ tab. Admins set up the customer portal domain and its [identity providers](/docs/platform/custom-identity-provider/) in the _Domain & Sign-in_ tab.
7. **Settings**: Manage the [secrets](/docs/controllers/secrets/) of your internal deployments in the _Secrets_ tab. Admins configure your organization name, registry [slug](/docs/registry/configuration/#1-set-your-registry-slug) and [custom domains](/docs/platform/branding/#custom-domains), and manage organization deletion. They add and manage the internal team members of your vendor organization in the _Team_ tab and view the current plan, usage limits and billing information in the _Subscription_ tab. With vendor billing enabled, they also manage license templates and the Stripe webhook in the _Billing_ tab.
8. **Account Menu**: Access your personal account settings from the dropdown in the top-right corner. Update your profile information or create [Personal Access Tokens](/docs/integrations/personal-access-token/) for API, SDK, and registry authentication to integrate Distr into your CI/CD pipelines.

## Command Palette

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on macOS) anywhere in the Vendor Portal to open the Command Palette. Type to search and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open the selected entry. The Command Palette lists:

- your applications, customers, deployments and artifacts
- deployment status filters, which open the deployments page filtered by that status
- every page of the sidebar
- the organizations you can switch to
- your customers under **Enter customer lens**, which opens their portal with [Customer Lens](/docs/platform/customer-lens/)

<ThemedImage
  lightSrc={commandPaletteLight}
  darkSrc={commandPaletteDark}

/>

Every sidebar page also has a keyboard shortcut: press <kbd>G</kbd> followed by the key of the page (<kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>H</kbd> for Home, <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>C</kbd> for Customers, <kbd>G</kbd> then <kbd>D</kbd> for Deployments). Hover over a sidebar entry to see its shortcut.
