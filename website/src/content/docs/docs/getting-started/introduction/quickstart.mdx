## Getting Started with Platform Tutorials

The fastest way to learn how to use Distr is through the interactive tutorials available directly on the platform. On the Community plan and during a trial, administrators see a **Getting started** card on the Home page with four guided tutorials that cover the core capabilities. If you hide the card, bring it back with **Show getting started**.

<ThemedImage
  lightSrc={tutorialsLight}
  darkSrc={tutorialsDark}

### 1. Invite Your Teammates

This tutorial lets you invite your colleagues, so you can manage your software distribution together. On plans with [user roles](/docs/platform/rbac/), it also explains the Administrator, User and Viewer roles and lets you pick one for each invitee.

### 2. Artifact Registry

This tutorial walks you through setting up your organization's [registry](/docs/registry/) slug, creating a personal access token and logging in, pushing and pulling artifacts from your own private artifact registry.

### 3. Controllers, Applications and Deployments Tutorial

In this tutorial, you'll learn how to download and install a [Distr controller](/docs/controllers/), deploy a sample hello-distr application, verify and monitor deployment statuses, and automate future releases.

### 4. Customer Portal and Onboarding Tutorial

This tutorial guides you through setting up your [Customer Portal](/docs/platform/customer-portal/) and inviting a customer. You'll learn how to customize the portal name and the getting started instructions, send the customer invite email, and preview the portal from the end customer's perspective.

## Accessing the Tutorials

To access these tutorials:

1. Create your **free** [Distr account](https://app.distr.sh/login)
2. Open the Home page
3. Pick a tutorial in the Getting started card
4. We recommend completing the tutorials in order for the best learning experience

## After Completing Tutorials

Upon completion, you'll be ready to:

1. **Create Your Own Applications:** Add your [Docker Compose or Helm chart-based applications](/docs/controllers/application/) to Distr
2. **Push Your Artifacts:** Store your artifacts in your private [registry](/docs/registry/)
3. **Invite Customers:** Invite new [customers](/docs/platform/rbac/#inviting-customer-organization-users) into your organization

### Next, explore the Use cases section

Visit the [Use cases](/docs/use-cases/fully-self-managed/) section to identify the scenario that best fits your end customers and begin implementing the most appropriate deployment model for them.
