[Bring Your Own Cloud (BYOC)](/glossary/byoc-definition/) is the vendor-operated pole of [self-hosted](/glossary/self-hosted-software/) (see [the self-hosted spectrum](/blog/self-hosted-spectrum/)): fully managed operations inside customer-owned cloud resources.
It suits organizations with strict data residency or compliance requirements that need to keep ownership of their cloud infrastructure.

BYOC runs on two independent Distr applications.

The first is an infrastructure application: a Docker Compose application that the [Docker agent](/docs/agents/docker-agent/)
deploys onto a small management VM in the end customer's cloud account. It reconciles their infrastructure on a loop and
creates the cluster and the resources your product needs. Its cloud credentials and its state stay in the customer's account.

The second is your application. The [Kubernetes agent](/docs/agents/kubernetes-agent/) manages it in the cluster the
infrastructure application created.

Both are versioned Distr applications, so an infrastructure change ships like any application update: publish a new version
and roll it out. This keeps every managed BYOC customer on the same infrastructure and application versions.

## Implementation steps

<Aside type="note">
  BYOC offers SaaS-like operational simplicity with the compliance benefits of
  customer resource ownership.
</Aside>

<Steps>

{/* steps can't be indented due to prettier */}

1. Set Up Your [Registry](/docs/registry/configuration/)
2. Create Your [Application](/docs/agents/application/)
3. White-label the [Customer portal](/docs/platform/branding/)
4. Onboard your first [customer](/docs/platform/rbac/#inviting-customer-organization-users)
5. Reach out to the Distr team to connect the customer's target environment _(We are working hard on automating this step)_
6. Manage deployments and updates of your app directly for the customer using the [Distr agents](/docs/agents/)

</Steps>
