Email Sending Provider

Send all transactional emails to your users and customers through your own SMTP server. Without a configuration, emails are sent by Distr. Make sure your SMTP server is authorized to send for the sender address domain (SPF, DKIM and DMARC), otherwise emails may be rejected or land in spam.
