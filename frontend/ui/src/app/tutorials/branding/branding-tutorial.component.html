Basic Branding for your Customer Portal

Before we can use the customer portal, we need to set it up. This tutorial will guide you through the process of setting up your Customer Portal and inviting a customer.

You can find additional resources on this topic in our docs.

  1. Branding Options

    Imagine you are one of your customers, getting started with self hosting your application: What would you like to see in your Customer Portal?

    Upload your logo and favicon, choose how the portal is named, write a short welcome page and set the browser tab title. All of this is optional and can be changed later on the Branding page, which also shows your custom domains.

  1. Invite Customer

    In this step you will invite a new customer and then log in to the Distr Customer Portal.

  2. A customer is a group of users inside your organization who have access to a shared set of deployments and cannot create applications or artifacts.
