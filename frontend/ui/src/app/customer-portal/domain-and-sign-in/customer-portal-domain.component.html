Domain & Sign-in

One domain that all of your customers sign in on by default. Without it they keep using your {{ appDomainLabel() }} or the default Distr host. Identity providers on it apply to every customer, including the ones you create later. A custom domain has to exist first.

Customers can override this with their own domain and providers on their settings page.
